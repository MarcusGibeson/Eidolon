# Eidolon v1234.9 Final Validation

## Scope

This source-only development checkpoint candidate completes **v1234.0-v1234.9 Requirement and Quality Assessment** under **Balanced Mind-and-Action Path 3**.

Eidolon can now prepare append-only, content-free assessments of structured development requirements and quality evidence using exact v1233 resource-assessment and operator-review lineage. An assessment may also bind an exact sealed v1229 execution outcome. Each requirement is classified deterministically as satisfied, partially satisfied, unsatisfied, unverified, contradictory, out of scope, or blocked by missing evidence.

The assessment records evidence coverage, ambiguity, remaining quality risks, rollback expectations, goal alignment, uncertainty, and bounded remediation proposal codes. Operator review supports accept assessment, hold, reject, request remediation, and request changes.

## Authority boundary

Assessment and review are evidence only. They do not:

- Approve or apply work.
- Modify requirements, projects, queues, schedules, source, or cognition.
- Launch, resume, pause, cancel, preempt, or retry a session.
- Contact a provider or execute commands or tests.
- Reuse consumed or historical authority.
- Install, promote, certify, release, or manage a model.

Accepting an assessment records only the operator's interpretation of the exact sealed assessment. Fresh separately governed authority remains mandatory for every later action.
