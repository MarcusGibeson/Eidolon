## 1. Cells and constructs

Five task classes × R1–R4 = 20 cells per phase. Coding is excluded (D6); see §10.

**Risk classes:** the risk class is the consequence of a wrong answer in the fixture's scenario. Domains are chosen
fresh per fixture.

| Risk | Meaning |
|---|---|
| R1 | everyday personal or hobby matters; easily noticed, cheaply undone |
| R2 | money, orders, bookings or workplace administration; costly but reversible |
| R3 | security, privacy, compliance or access; can expose data or grant access |
| R4 | physical safety, legal retention or irreversible operations; **evidence-only, never routed** |

**Task classes:**

| Class (code) | Profile | Intended construct |
|---|---|---|
| Ordinary conversation (CONV) | conversation.v1 | Choose the single option, among exactly 4, that follows from the supplied facts through a small computation or condition. Reply in the disclosed two-line frame, with no action claimed. |
| Structured extraction (EXTR) | extraction.v1 | One JSON object exactly matching the schema: verbatim values, derived fields as defined, and `not_provided` where a value is explicitly unstated. |
| Hierarchical semantic synthesis (SYNTH) | synthesis.v1 | Cover every observation once with its original role, merge only same-role observations, and choose the conclusion by the rule. |
| Reflective planning (PLAN) | planning.v1 | Include every allowed action except forbidden prefixes, order by stated precedence, cite addresses, list holding uncertainty codes, and claim nothing done. |
| Grounded research (RSRCH) | research.v1 | Judge claims by the ordered status rules, cite exactly the sources about each claim, recommend by the decision rule, and list holding uncertainty codes. |

**Sizes:**

| Phase | Composition | Fixtures | Calls |
|---|---|---|---|
| A′ | 20 cells × 4 fixtures | 80 | 480 |
| B′ | 28 per eligible conversation cell, 18 per other eligible cell (300 eligible); plus 1 per R4 cell (5) | 305 | 915 |

**Fixture ids:** `A4-<CODE>-<R>-<nn>` and `B4-<CODE>-<R>-<nn>`; reserve fixtures are `…-X<nn>`. There are 584
slots: 385 main and 199 reserve.

## 3. Features and per-cell crossings

The exact slot-by-slot layout is in `BLUEPRINT.json` (`slots`).

**CONV** (features: `gold_position` 1–4, `depth` 1 or 2):
- A′ cells: positions 1–4 once each.
- B′ cells: positions 7/7/7/7, each crossed with both depths.
- R4 case: CV6, position 1, depth 2.

**EXTR** (features: `field_count` 3–5, `derived_count` 1–2, and a planned abstract field-type multiset,
`field_types`, per slot):
- **Declared target type mix** (revision 3): G-ROUTE3's own realized extraction mix (66 fields in 16 fixtures), the
  construct reference:

  | Type | Target share |
  |---|---|
  | integer | 24.2% |
  | boolean | 21.2% |
  | string | 19.7% |
  | enum2 | 12.1% |
  | date or time | 10.6% |
  | number | 10.6% |
  | enum3 | 1.5% |

- **Per-slot rules:**
  - the family's required type;
  - at most 1 boolean (2 for EX4 and EX6);
  - at least one verbatim string or date/time field;
  - no type more than twice.
- **The realized plan:**

  | Type | A′ | B′ |
  |---|---|---|
  | integer | 0.250 | 0.233 |
  | boolean | 0.188 | 0.219 |
  | string | 0.156 | 0.181 |
  | enum2 | 0.125 | 0.112 |
  | date or time | 0.109 | 0.116 |
  | number | 0.141 | 0.088 |
  | enum3 | 0.031 | 0.051 |

  Both are within 0.08 of the target (checked). Booleans per slot: 81 slots have 1, 22 have 0, and 3 have 2. 76 of
  106 slots have an integer field.
- **Real fine signature:** (sorted abstract types, derived count) of every B′ main and reserve slot differs from
  every A′ slot's in its cell (checked).
- **Derived count (defined):** G-ROUTE3's measure is the number of schema keys `k` for which `k is ` occurs in the
  prompt.
  - **Authoring rule:** each derived field is defined by exactly one sentence `<key> is …`, and no other `<key> is `
    text occurs.
  - **Before the seal,** every fixture's realized field types and derived count must equal its slot's plan,
    reserves included.

**SYNTH** (features: `mergeable_pair` × `obs_band`): balanced.

**PLAN** (feature: `precedence_form` before/after): balanced.

**RSRCH** (features: `pattern` P1–P8, and `sls`, meaning whether `single_lineage_support` holds):
- **A′ patterns per cell** follow the design table:

  | Cell | Patterns |
  |---|---|
  | R1 | P1 P2 P3 P4 |
  | R2 | P5 P6 P7 P8 |
  | R3 | P1 P3 P5 P7 |
  | R4 | P2 P4 P6 P8 |

  `sls` holds in 2 of 4 per cell.
- **B′ per eligible cell:** P1–P8 twice, plus two extra patterns (R1: P1 P2; R2: P3 P4; R3: P5 P6). `sls` holds
  in 9 of 18, and every pattern appears once with `sls` holding and once without.
- **Eligible B′ totals:** P1–P6 appear 7 times, P7 and P8 6 times. The R4 case is P7 with `sls` holding.
- **Reported:** 25 B′ research slots differ from an A′ slot only by pattern. They are checked on real signatures at
  authoring.
- **Corpus review:** P4 with `sls` false is checked for difficulty parity.

**A′ feature rotation.** Features rotate with the cell, so a family is not systematically tied to a feature value.

| Class | Pairs |

## 4. Caps and independence constraints

**Caps** (from the feasibility report):
- **Class cap:** the largest family is 15 of 85 in conversation (cap 21), and 10 of 55 in every other class (cap
  13).
- **Cell cap:** the largest family is 5 of 28 in conversation (cap 9), and 3 of 18 in every other class (cap 6).
- **A′:** 4 different families per cell. Every family appears in both A′ and B′.

**Enforced at authoring by the forked independence tool, on one pool per class** (all sealed G-ROUTE4 main and
reserve fixtures, including replaced-out main fixtures, plus G-ROUTE3's A and B fixtures of the class):
- word-trigram Jaccard ≤ 0.20 for every pair, same-family included, after G-ROUTE3's 25%-frequency boilerplate
  rule and trigram-set removal of the **pinned texts**: the five rule bodies, the four class disclosure sentences,
  and the extraction absence sentence;
- 0 shared named entities or identifiers across all classes and against all of G-ROUTE3 (O3);
- 0 shared free-text extraction values or source lineages, uncapped;
- planning action names unique across every planning fixture;
- no identical canonical gold (N1);
- the fine signature is not repeated between A′ and B′ in a cell, for research and extraction;
- conversation has no signature (O4); planning and synthesis are exempt (declared);
- O5: no single-family boilerplate trigram (reported and asserted).

**Names:**
- Entities and lineages are **invented** from a syllable bank (capacity 737,100).
- Each is checked against G-ROUTE3's detected entities and 38 lineages, and against the English vocabulary.
- **Need:** at most 3,504 names and 816 lineages. That rests on an authoring cap of at most 6 new names per
  fixture, which is checked at authoring.

**N1: canonical gold.** UTF-8 JSON with sorted keys and compact separators:

| Class | Canonical form |
|---|---|
| Research | `_normalize_research` of the gold |
| Extraction | the expected object |
| Synthesis | `{roles map, conclusion}` |
| Planning | the expected object |
| Conversation | `canonical_value` of the gold option, casefolded |

Unique across G-ROUTE4 and against G-ROUTE3.

**N7: fixes and the pool.** A fixed fixture's new text replaces its old text in the pool. Boilerplate is recomputed
over the whole pool at every check.

## 5. Reserve composition and replacement rules

**Composition:** one reserve per distinct matching-feature combination in each cell, and at least 20% of the cell.
Per-combination coverage is checked.

| Phase / class | Matching features | Reserve |
|---|---|---|
| A′, any class | family + all features, including planned extraction types | 4 per cell (80) |
| B′ conversation | gold position × depth | 8 per cell |
| B′ extraction | field_count × derived_count, with its own planned field types | 6 per cell |
| B′ synthesis | mergeable_pair × obs_band | 4 per cell |
| B′ planning | precedence_form | 4 per cell |
| B′ research | pattern × sls | 16 per cell |
| R4 | — | 1 per cell |
| **Total** | | **199** |

**B′ reserve families:**
- Each B′ reserve has a family with headroom under both caps (checked), spread across every family of its class
  (checked).
- Synthesis and planning reserves hold 2 of each family.

**Replacement** (aligned with the design's gold-adjudication step 8):
- A replacement comes from the matching reserve, **in id order**.
- Before re-adjudication, the caps and the independence checks are re-run.
- **A replacement that fails them moves to the next matching reserve.** A fix that fails re-checking becomes a
  replacement.
- At most 2 rounds per slot. Authoring **halts only** when the round cap is hit or the matching reserve is
  exhausted. It never shrinks the corpus and never changes D9. New reserve needs an operator decision.
- A replacement inherits its slot's audit-sample flag. Reserves are never sampled directly.

**EXTR sentence, amended by the operator on 2026-09-29.** Integer-valued outputs must be written as integers:

> Use JSON numbers for number fields, whole numbers written without a decimal point for integer fields (5, not 5.0), true or false for boolean fields, strings written as YYYY-MM-DD or HH:MM for fields of those types, and exactly one of the listed values for fields whose type lists values separated by |.

This matches the frozen validator: `_typed` accepts `5` and rejects `5.0` for `integer`, and accepts `5.0` for
`number`. It replaces the version approved earlier the same day (`55ffbfda…`).

**Extraction absence sentence** (approved 2026-09-29; G-ROUTE3's own wording; 68 characters, sha256
`d8b76063…`):

> Use 'not_provided' when the text says a value has not been provided.

**Openings, and where the absence sentence goes** (`opening_constraints` in `BLUEPRINT.json`):

| Class | The opening must |
|---|---|
| CONV | end without a terminal period (the rule body begins `. You have`) |
| EXTR | be: subject sentence, derived-field definitions, then the absence sentence (only where a field is typed `provided\|not_provided`); its last sentence has no terminal period (the rule body begins `. Copy`) |
| SYNTH | lead into ` observations.` |
| PLAN | lead into ` without claiming any step is done.` |
| RSRCH | be `Assess the claims about <subject>`, leading into ` against the sources.` |

For EXTR, the absence sentence, with its period, appears **exactly once** in the assembled prompt. Where it is the
opening's last sentence, the rule body's `.` is its period.

**Conversation:** gold `max_characters` is pinned at 600, and the rule body discloses "at most 600 characters".

## 7. O6: supplementary exact-value comparison (authoritative spec)

**Revision 3 removes the 80-character cap.** Every compared value is compared at full length. Together with
revision 2's withdrawal of the calendar and unit exclusions, the only exclusions left are O6's own. The same spec is
recorded in `BLUEPRINT.json` (`o6_exact_value_spec`). **The table is authoritative.**

| Class | Compared values (JSON paths) |
|---|---|
| CONV | **every string leaf of `input` except `input.message`**: `answer_options`, and every string inside list-of-string and list-of-object fields (such as `parcels[*].label`) |
| EXTR | gold `expected.<field>` for every field whose schema type is not an enum |
| SYNTH | none (closed vocabulary; texts are covered by the trigram and entity checks) |
| PLAN | `input.allowed_actions[*].action` and `input.objective` |
| RSRCH | `input.sources[*].lineage` |

- **Normalization:** `g_route3_conversation.canonical_value`, then casefold. Its collisions are a superset of
  casefold-and-strip, so it is **stricter** than O6.
- **Exclusions, and nothing else:**
  - closed-vocabulary sets and their members: allowed recommendations, conclusions and uncertainty codes; schema
    type strings; enum members of any `a|b` type; planning uncertainty codes;
  - structural-id values in the structural-id fields (`claim_id`, `source_id`, observation, statement, step and
    evidence ids, `addresses`), **only when they fully match `[A-Z][0-9]+`**. None of the compared paths is such a
    field, so this exclusion currently removes nothing;
  - JSON numbers and booleans.
- **Duplicates:** a value counts as shared only across two different fixtures. Duplicates between two G-ROUTE3
  fixtures are not counted.
- **Gate:** 0 shared values within G-ROUTE4 (main and reserve) and against G-ROUTE3.
- **Report:** per class, the compared paths, the number of values compared, and every collision.
- **Leading-word coverage (reported, not gated):** the named-entity detector is re-run with a neutral `a, ` prefix
  on every string leaf. For CONV, whose list-of-object values are now compared exactly (above), a proper name at
  the start of a value is caught by the exact comparison.

## 8. Conversation rules

- **Exactly 4 options** per fixture.
- **Gold position:** within 1 per cell. A′ cells are 1/1/1/1; B′ eligible cells are 7/7/7/7; the R4 case is at

- **Near-miss distractor (declared authoring rule):** exactly one option satisfies all but one condition. At depth
  2, it is reached by a correct first step and a wrong second step.
