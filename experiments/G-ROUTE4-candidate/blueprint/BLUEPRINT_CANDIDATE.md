## 1. Cells and constructs

Five task classes × R1–R4 = 20 cells per phase. Coding is excluded (D6); see §10.

**Risk classes:** the risk class is the consequence of a wrong answer in the fixture's scenario. Domains are chosen
fresh per fixture to fit their risk class.

| Risk | Meaning |
|---|---|
| R1 | everyday personal or hobby matters; easily noticed, cheaply undone |
| R2 | money, orders, bookings or workplace administration; costly but reversible |
| R3 | security, privacy, compliance or access; can expose data or grant access |
| R4 | physical safety, legal retention or irreversible operations; **evidence-only, never routed** |

**Task classes:**

| Class (code) | Profile | Intended construct |
|---|---|---|
| Ordinary conversation (CONV) | conversation.v1 | Choose the single option, among exactly 4, that follows from the supplied facts through a small computation or condition. Reply in the disclosed two-line frame, with no action claimed. |
| Structured extraction (EXTR) | extraction.v1 | One JSON object exactly matching the schema: verbatim values, derived fields as defined, and `not_provided` where a value is explicitly unstated. |
| Hierarchical semantic synthesis (SYNTH) | synthesis.v1 | Cover every observation once with its original role, merge only same-role observations, and choose the conclusion by the rule. |
| Reflective planning (PLAN) | planning.v1 | Include every allowed action except forbidden prefixes, order by stated precedence, cite addresses, list holding uncertainty codes, and claim nothing done. |
| Grounded research (RSRCH) | research.v1 | Judge claims by the ordered status rules, cite exactly the sources about each claim, recommend by the decision rule, and list holding uncertainty codes. |

**Sizes:**

| Phase | Composition | Fixtures | Calls |
|---|---|---|---|
| A′ | 20 cells × 4 fixtures | 80 | 480 |
| B′ | eligible cells: 28 per conversation R1–R3 cell, 18 per other R1–R3 cell (300 eligible); plus 1 per R4 cell (5) | 305 | 915 |

**Fixture ids:** main fixtures are `A4-<CODE>-<R>-<nn>` and `B4-<CODE>-<R>-<nn>`; reserve fixtures are
`…-X<nn>`. There are 584 slots in total: 80 + 305 main, 199 reserve.

## 2. Template families (six per class; structure only)

**A family is a structure, not wording.** Every family's model-facing template text is **empty**. The only fixed
model-facing text per class is the pinned **rule body** and its frozen disclosure sentence (§6). Every fixture's
opening, and all its data, is written fresh.

Families also carry no G-ROUTE3 text. PL1 has the *same counts* as G-ROUTE3's planning shape, but none of its text.

**Conversation:**

| Family | Structure |
|---|---|
| CV1 | two-condition filter |
| CV2 | date or clock-time arithmetic |
| CV3 | unit conversion, then a comparison |
| CV4 | conditional count or aggregation |
| CV5 | percentage or threshold |
| CV6 | rule with a stated exception or precedence |

**Extraction:**

| Family | Structure | Always includes |
|---|---|---|

| EX3 | numeric aggregation | a number field |
| EX4 | threshold or compliance | a boolean field |
| EX5 | explicit absence | a `provided\|not_provided` field |

**Synthesis:**

**Placement rules:**

## 3. Features and per-cell crossings

The exact slot-by-slot layout is in `BLUEPRINT.json` (`slots`).

**CONV** (features: `gold_position` 1–4, `depth` 1 or 2):
- A′ cells: positions 1–4 once each.
- B′ cells: positions 7/7/7/7, each crossed with both depths.
- R4 case: CV6, position 1, depth 2.

**EXTR** (features: `field_count` 3–5, `derived_count` 1–2, and a **planned abstract field-type multiset**,
`field_types`, per slot):
- The types are G-ROUTE3's abstract types: string, integer, number, boolean, date_or_time, enum2, enum3.
- **The real fine signature** (sorted abstract types, derived count) of every B′ main and reserve slot differs from
  every A′ slot's in its cell. The feasibility report checks this on the planned types, not on a proxy.

**SYNTH** (features: `mergeable_pair` yes/no × `obs_band` 3–4 / 5–6 observations): balanced.

**PLAN** (feature: `precedence_form` before/after): balanced.

**RSRCH** (features: `pattern` P1–P8, and `sls`, meaning whether `single_lineage_support` holds):
- **A′ patterns per cell** follow the design table:

  | Cell | Patterns |
  |---|---|
  | R1 | P1 P2 P3 P4 |
  | R2 | P5 P6 P7 P8 |
  | R3 | P1 P3 P5 P7 |
  | R4 | P2 P4 P6 P8 |

  `sls` holds in 2 of 4 per cell.
- **B′ per eligible cell:** P1–P8 twice, plus two extra patterns (R1: P1 P2; R2: P3 P4; R3: P5 P6). `sls` holds
  in 9 of 18, and **every pattern appears once with `sls` holding and once without**.
- **Eligible B′ totals:** P1–P6 appear 7 times, P7 and P8 6 times. The R4 case is P7 with `sls` holding.
- **Reported:** 27 B′ research slots (main and reserve) share (family, `sls`) with an A′ slot and differ from it
  only by pattern. Pattern changes statuses and codes, so their real signatures should differ. This is checked on
  the real signatures at authoring.
- **Corpus review:** P4 with `sls` false places the same-lineage repetition on a claim that is not
  single-lineage-supported; its difficulty parity is checked at the corpus review.

## 4. Caps and independence constraints

**Caps** (from the feasibility report):
- **Class cap** (≤ 25% of a class's B′ cases, R4 counted):

  | Class | Largest family | Cap |
  |---|---|---|
  | Conversation | 15 of 85 | 21 |
  | Extraction, synthesis, planning | 10 of 55 | 13 |
  | Research | 11 of 55 | 13 |

- **Cell cap** (≤ one third of an eligible B′ cell):

  | Cell | Largest family | Cap |
  |---|---|---|
  | Conversation | 5 of 28 | 9 |
  | Extraction, synthesis, planning | 3 of 18 | 6 |
  | Research R1, R3 | 6 of 18 | 6 (at the cap) |
  | Research R2 | 5 of 18 | 6 |

- **A′:** 4 different families per cell. Every family appears in both A′ and B′.

**Enforced at authoring by the forked independence tool, on one pool per class** (all sealed G-ROUTE4 main and
reserve fixtures, **including replaced-out main fixtures, which stay in the pool once sealed**, plus G-ROUTE3's A
and B fixtures of the class):
- word-trigram Jaccard ≤ 0.20 for every pair, same-family included, after G-ROUTE3's 25%-frequency boilerplate rule
  and trigram-set removal of the pinned rule bodies and disclosure sentences;
- 0 shared named entities or identifiers **across all classes** and against all of G-ROUTE3 (O3);
- 0 shared free-text extraction values or source lineages, **uncapped in length**;
- planning action names unique across every planning fixture (stricter than the design's action-set exclusion,
  which is not used);
- no identical canonical gold (N1);
- the fine signature is not repeated between A′ and B′ in a cell, for research and extraction;
- conversation has no signature (O4); planning and synthesis are exempt (declared);
- **O5:** the forked tool reports every boilerplate trigram that occurs in a family, and asserts that none is
  specific to a single family.

**Names:**
- Entities and lineage names are **invented** from a syllable bank (capacity 737,100).
- Each is checked against G-ROUTE3's 28 detected entities and 38 lineages, and against the English lowercase
  vocabulary.
- **Need:** at most 3,504 names for 584 fixtures, and 816 lineages for 136 research fixtures.
- **Canonical gold must also be unique** (N1). Numeric conversation answers and the small synthesis answer space
  (roles map plus conclusion) need care.

**N1: canonical gold.** UTF-8 JSON with sorted keys and compact separators:

| Class | Canonical form |
|---|---|
| Research | `_normalize_research` of the gold |
| Extraction | the expected object |
| Synthesis | `{roles map, conclusion}` |
| Planning | the expected object |
| Conversation | `canonical_value` of the gold option, casefolded |

The same form is recorded in `BLUEPRINT.json` (`n1_canonical_gold`).

**N7: fixes and the pool.** A fixed fixture's new text replaces its old text in the pool. Boilerplate is recomputed
over the whole pool at every check: before the seal, after every fix or replacement, and on the final corpus.

## 5. Reserve composition and replacement rules

**Composition:** one reserve fixture per distinct matching-feature combination present in each cell, and at least
20% of the cell. Coverage of every combination is checked explicitly.

| Phase / class | Matching features | Reserve |
|---|---|---|
| A′, any class | family + all features (a replacement keeps its slot's family) | 4 per cell (80) |
| B′ conversation | gold position × depth | 8 per cell |
| B′ extraction | field_count × derived_count | 6 per cell |
| B′ synthesis | mergeable_pair × obs_band | 4 per cell |
| B′ planning | precedence_form | 4 per cell |
| B′ research | pattern × sls | 16 per cell |
| R4 | — | 1 per cell |
| **Total** | | **199** |

**B′ reserve families** (revision 2):
- Each B′ reserve has a family, spread across every family with headroom (at most 4 of one family per research
  cell).
- Headroom means any single replacement keeps both caps.
- Multiple replacements are guarded by the per-replacement cap re-check at authoring; a failure halts.
- Extraction reserves carry planned field-type multisets under the same signature rule.

**Replacement** (design gold-adjudication steps 3–10, O1 and O2):
- A replacement comes from the matching reserve, in id order.
- Caps are re-checked, and the independence checks re-run, before re-adjudication.
- A fix that fails re-checking becomes a replacement.
- At most 2 rounds per slot. If the round cap is hit or the matching reserve is exhausted, authoring **halts**. It
  never shrinks the corpus and never changes D9. New reserve needs an operator decision.
- A replacement inherits its slot's audit-sample flag. Reserves are never sampled directly.

## 7. O6: supplementary exact-value comparison (authoritative spec)

**Revision 2 withdraws the candidate's calendar, weekday and number-with-unit exclusions.** They went beyond O6.
Dates, clock times, weekday names and amount strings are now compared like any other string. The same spec is
recorded in `BLUEPRINT.json` (`o6_exact_value_spec`). **The table is authoritative.**

| Class | Compared values (JSON paths) |
|---|---|
| CONV | `input.answer_options[*]`, and every entry of any other list-of-strings input field |
| EXTR | gold `expected.<field>` for every field whose schema type is not an enum |
| SYNTH | none (structured fields are closed vocabulary; texts are covered by the trigram and entity checks) |
| PLAN | `input.allowed_actions[*].action` |
| RSRCH | `input.sources[*].lineage` |

- **Normalization:** `g_route3_conversation.canonical_value`, then casefold.
- **Exclusions, and nothing else:**
  - closed-vocabulary sets and their members: allowed recommendations, conclusions and uncertainty codes; schema
    type strings; enum members of any `a|b` type; planning uncertainty codes;
  - structural-id values, **only when they fully match `[A-Z][0-9]+`**;
  - JSON numbers and booleans.
- **Duplicates:** a value counts as shared only across two different fixtures.
- **Length:** the 80-character cap applies **only** to this supplementary comparison. The design's shared-value
  checks (§4) run uncapped.
- **Gate:** 0 shared values within G-ROUTE4 (main and reserve) and against G-ROUTE3 under the same rule.
- **Leading-word coverage (reported, not gated):** the named-entity detector is re-run with a neutral `a, ` prefix
  on every string leaf.

## 8. Conversation rules

- **Exactly 4 options** per fixture.
- **Gold position:** within 1 per cell. A′ cells are 1/1/1/1; B′ eligible cells are 7/7/7/7; the R4 case is at
  position 1. Reserves match on position.
- **Options** are pairwise distinct under `canonical_value`.
- **Near-miss distractor (declared authoring rule):** exactly one option satisfies all but one condition. At depth
  2, it is the option reached by a correct first step and a wrong second step. It is not a design construct change:
  G-ROUTE3's conversation options already included plausible wrong answers.

## 9. Research feature balance

See §3.
- P1–P8 are the construct patterns, relabelled from G-ROUTE3's S1–S8.
- `sls` holds in exactly half of every eligible cell, and crosses every pattern in B′.
- The allocation comes from the construct.
- The declared coverage gap (A′ 4 patterns per cell, B′ 8) is in the design.

## 10. Coding (excluded; forward specification only)

This section is unchanged from revision 1. No G-ROUTE4 slot is coding.

## 12. Audit-sample procedure (O1), frozen with the blueprint

`audit_sample.py`:
- `key = sha256(ascii(seal_commit_id) + ":" + ascii(fixture_id))`.
- For each B′ cell, the `ceil(0.10 × size)` main fixtures with the smallest key are sampled, **38 in total**.
- A replacement inherits its slot's flag. Reserves are never sampled directly.
- **Frozen test vector:** `75af7d1ad5d785b40c1a41f15e4d23fa7e987715530e8d9b64f4fec5a4b398c9`.
- **Runbook addition** (for the seal stage, not a change to O1):
  - the single seal commit is **pushed to `origin` before the sample is computed**;
  - the local reflog around the seal is disclosed in the adjudication records, so re-rolling candidate seal
    commits would be visible.
