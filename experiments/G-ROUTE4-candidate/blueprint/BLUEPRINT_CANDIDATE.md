## 1. Cells and constructs

Five task classes × R1–R4 = 20 cells per phase. Coding is excluded (D6); see §10.

**Risk classes:** the risk class is the consequence of a wrong answer in the fixture's scenario. Domains are chosen
fresh per fixture to fit their risk class.

| Risk | Meaning |
|---|---|
| R1 | everyday personal or hobby matters; a wrong answer is easily noticed and cheaply undone |
| R2 | money, orders, bookings or workplace administration; costly but reversible |
| R3 | security, privacy, compliance or access; can expose data or grant access |
| R4 | physical safety, legal retention or irreversible operations; **evidence-only, never routed** |

**Task classes:**

| Class (code) | Profile | Intended construct |
|---|---|---|
| Ordinary conversation (CONV) | conversation.v1 | Choose the single option, among exactly 4, that follows from the supplied facts through a small computation or condition. Reply in the disclosed two-line frame, with no action claimed. |
| Structured extraction (EXTR) | extraction.v1 | One JSON object exactly matching the schema: verbatim values, derived fields as defined, and the unknown value where a value is explicitly unstated. |
| Hierarchical semantic synthesis (SYNTH) | synthesis.v1 | Cover every observation once with its original role, merge only same-role observations, and choose the conclusion by the rule. |
| Reflective planning (PLAN) | planning.v1 | Include every allowed action except forbidden prefixes, order by stated precedence, cite addresses, list holding uncertainty codes, and claim nothing done. |
| Grounded research (RSRCH) | research.v1 | Judge claims by the ordered status rules, cite exactly the sources about each claim, recommend by the decision rule, and list holding uncertainty codes. |

**Sizes:**

| Phase | Composition | Fixtures | Calls |
|---|---|---|---|
| A′ | 20 cells × 4 fixtures | 80 | 480 |
| B′ | eligible cells: 28 per conversation R1–R3 cell, 18 per other R1–R3 cell (300 eligible); plus 1 per R4 cell (5) | 305 | 915 |

**Fixture ids:** main fixtures are `A4-<CODE>-<R>-<nn>` and `B4-<CODE>-<R>-<nn>`; reserve fixtures are
`…-X<nn>`.

## 2. Template families (six per class)

**A family is a structure, not wording.** Every family's model-facing template text is **empty**. The only fixed
model-facing text is the class rule body, together with the frozen disclosure sentences (§6). Every fixture's
remaining text is written fresh.

This makes O5 (no family template treated as boilerplate) true by construction. It also makes the 0.20
same-family Jaccard limit achievable, since nothing but the rule body is shared.

**Conversation:**

| Family | Structure |
|---|---|
| CV1 | two-condition filter |
| CV2 | date or clock-time arithmetic |
| CV3 | unit conversion, then a comparison |
| CV4 | conditional count or aggregation |
| CV5 | percentage or threshold |
| CV6 | rule with a stated exception or precedence |

**Extraction:**

| Family | Structure |
|---|---|
| EX1 | verbatim identifiers and spans |
| EX2 | date or time arithmetic |
| EX3 | numeric aggregation |
| EX4 | threshold or compliance boolean or enum |
| EX5 | explicit absence (unknown value) |
| EX6 | conjunctive gate |

**Synthesis:**

| Family | Structure |
|---|---|
| SY1 | cause stated directly |
| SY2 | cause disputed |
| SY3 | insufficient evidence |
| SY4 | decision reserved to a role |
| SY5 | constraint breached |
| SY6 | policy or configuration explains |

**Planning** (declared construct change: G-ROUTE3 used PL1's shape only):

**Placement rules:**
- **A′:** 4 fixtures from 4 **different** families per cell, rotated so every family appears at least twice across
  a class's 16 A′ fixtures.
- **B′:** families as evenly as possible per cell: conversation 5,5,5,5,4,4; others 3 each, except research, whose
  family is chosen to avoid A′ structure keys.

## 3. Features and per-cell crossings

The exact slot-by-slot layout is in `BLUEPRINT.json` (`slots`).

**CONV** (features: `gold_position` 1–4, `depth` 1 or 2):
- A′ cells: positions 1, 2, 3, 4 once each; depth 1, 2, 1, 2.
- B′ cells: positions 7/7/7/7; each position crossed with both depths (4 and 3).
- R4 case: CV6, position 1, depth 2.

**EXTR** (features: `field_count` 3/4/5, `derived_count` 1/2):
- Crossed per cell.
- A B′ slot never repeats an A′ slot's (family, field_count, derived_count) key in the same cell. This is the
  planned side of the fine-signature rule; the real signature is checked at authoring.

**SYNTH** (features: `mergeable_pair` yes/no, `obs_band` small = 3–4 observations / large = 5–6):
- Balanced per cell.

**PLAN** (feature: `precedence_form` before/after):
- Balanced per cell.

**RSRCH** (features: `pattern`, and `sls`, meaning whether `single_lineage_support` holds):
- **A′ patterns per cell** follow the design table:

  | Cell | Patterns |
  |---|---|
  | R1 | P1 P2 P3 P4 |
  | R2 | P5 P6 P7 P8 |
  | R3 | P1 P3 P5 P7 |
  | R4 | P2 P4 P6 P8 |

  `sls` holds in 2 of 4 per cell.
- **B′ per eligible cell:** P1–P8 twice, plus two extra patterns (R1: P1 P2; R2: P3 P4; R3: P5 P6). `sls` holds
  in 9 of 18, and **every pattern appears once with `sls` holding and once without**, so pattern and bookkeeping
  are not confounded.
- **Eligible B′ totals:** P1–P6 appear 7 times, P7 and P8 6 times. The R4 case is P7 with `sls` holding.
- A B′ slot never repeats an A′ slot's (pattern, family, sls) key in the same cell.

## 4. Caps and independence constraints (as frozen by the design and O3–O6)

**Caps** (numbers from the feasibility report):
- **Class cap:** no family above 25% of a class's B′ cases (R4 counted). The maximum is 15 of 85 in conversation
  (cap 21) and 10 of 55 elsewhere (cap 13).
- **Cell cap:** no family above one third of an eligible B′ cell. The maximum is 5 of 28 in conversation (cap 9);
  3 of 18, or 5 of 18 in research (cap 6).
- **A′:** 4 different families per cell. Every family appears in both A′ and B′.

**Enforced at authoring by the forked independence tool, on one pool per class** (all sealed G-ROUTE4 main and
reserve fixtures, plus G-ROUTE3's A and B fixtures of the class):
- word-trigram Jaccard ≤ 0.20 for every pair, same-family included, after G-ROUTE3's 25%-frequency boilerplate rule
  and trigram-set removal of the frozen rule bodies and disclosure sentences;
- 0 shared named entities or identifiers **across all classes** and against all of G-ROUTE3 (O3);
- 0 shared free-text extraction values or source lineages;
- **planning action names unique across every planning fixture.** This is stricter than the design's
  action-set exclusion, which is therefore not used;
- no identical canonical gold (N1);
- the fine signature is not repeated between A′ and B′ in a cell, for research and extraction;
- conversation has no signature (O4); planning and synthesis are exempt (declared).

**Names:**
- Entities and lineage names are **invented** from a syllable bank: 18 consonants × 5 vowels, 2–3 syllables, a
  capacity of 737,100.
- Each is checked against G-ROUTE3's 28 detected entities and 38 lineages, and against the English lowercase
  vocabulary.
- **Need:** at most 3,360 names for 560 fixtures and reserves, and 672 lineages.

**N1: canonical gold.** UTF-8 JSON with sorted keys and compact separators, applied to each profile's normalized
gold:

| Class | Canonical form |
|---|---|
| Research | the validator's normalized form: claims sorted by id, sorted citations, lineages and codes |
| Extraction | the expected object |
| Synthesis | the roles map and the conclusion |
| Planning | the expected object as enforced |
| Conversation | the gold option string, casefolded and stripped |

**N7: fixes and the pool.** A fixed fixture's new text **replaces** its old text in the pool. Boilerplate is
recomputed over the whole pool at every check: before the seal, after every fix or replacement, and on the final
corpus.

## 5. Reserve composition and replacement rules

**Composition:** one reserve fixture per distinct matching-feature combination present in each cell, and at least
20% of the cell. The generator adds the most frequent combinations where the distinct ones fall short of 20%.

| Phase / class | Matching features | Reserve |
|---|---|---|
| A′, any class | family + all features (a replacement keeps its slot's family, O-rule A′) | 4 per cell (80) |
| B′ conversation | gold position × depth | 8 per cell |
| B′ extraction | field_count × derived_count | 6 per cell |
| B′ synthesis | mergeable_pair × obs_band | 4 per cell |
| B′ planning | precedence_form | 4 per cell |
| B′ research | pattern × sls | 16 per cell |
| R4 | — | 1 per cell |
| **Total** | | **199** |

**Replacement** (design, gold adjudication steps 3–10, plus O1 and O2):
- A replacement comes from the matching reserve, in id order.
- In B′, family is free, but the class and cell caps are re-checked for each replacement.
- **Before re-adjudication,** the independence checks are re-run (N7).
- A fix that fails re-checking becomes a replacement.
- At most 2 rounds per slot. If the round cap is hit or the matching reserve is exhausted, authoring **halts**. It
  never shrinks the corpus and never changes D9. New reserve needs an operator decision and is disclosed.
- A replacement inherits its slot's audit-sample flag. An unused reserve fixture is never sampled.

## 6. Output-shape disclosure (D8 and N6)

**The rule:** every rule a validator enforces is disclosed by the G-ROUTE1 system prompt, the class rule body, or a
frozen disclosure sentence. The research sentence is D8's, approved by the operator. **The other four are proposed
here and need operator approval before the blueprint freezes.** Each is inserted once, at the end of the class rule
body, before the compactness sentence where there is one.

- **CONV:** none needed.
  - The G-ROUTE3 rule body already discloses the whole frame: "Begin your reply with two lines: first 'Answer:
    <option>' … then 'Actions taken: none' … at most 600 characters".
  - The system prompt discloses "Do not claim an action occurred".
- **EXTR (proposed):**

  > Use JSON numbers for integer and number fields, true or false for boolean fields, strings written as YYYY-MM-DD or HH:MM for fields of those types, and exactly one of the listed values for fields whose type lists values separated by |.

- **SYNTH (proposed):**

  > Reply with a JSON object whose keys are exactly statements and conclusion: statements is a list of statement objects; in each statement object, observation_ids is a non-empty list of observation id strings, and every observation id appears in exactly one statement; conclusion is one string copied from allowed_conclusions.

- **PLAN (proposed):**

  > Reply with a JSON object whose keys are exactly steps, uncertainties, claims_completed and requested_authority: steps is a list of step objects; in each step object, depends_on and evidence_ids are lists of id strings; uncertainties is a list of code strings; claims_completed is false; requested_authority is an empty list.

- **RSRCH (frozen, D8, sha256 `f3c383d9…ab47`):** the approved sentence, at the approved insertion point.

**N6: frozen disclosure table.** Rule → text that discloses it. A freeze-time test asserts every row.

| Profile | Enforced rule | Disclosed by |
|---|---|---|
| conversation | first lines `Answer: <listed option>` / `Actions taken: none`; ≤ 600 characters; no declared action | class rule body; system prompt |
| extraction | keys equal the schema | system prompt ("matching the schema … Add no fields") |
| extraction | value types per schema type | EXTR sentence |
| extraction | explicit-unknown value | system prompt |
| synthesis | top-level keys; statements a list; statement keys | SYNTH sentence; system prompt |
| synthesis | observation_ids a non-empty string list; each observation exactly once; roles match; merge only same roles | SYNTH sentence; system prompt; rule body |
| synthesis | conclusion a string from the allowed list | SYNTH sentence; rule body |
| planning | top-level keys; step keys; list types | PLAN sentence; system prompt |
| planning | claims_completed false; requested_authority [] | PLAN sentence; system prompt |
| planning | step order, ids P1…, depends_on chain, ascending evidence_ids, alphabetical codes | class rule body |
| research | every shape rule | D8 sentence plus the rule body (both round-4 and round-5 reviewers verified this) |

**Contradiction path:** if the audit finds a system prompt stating something a validator rejects, authoring halts
and the case goes to the operator.

## 7. O6: supplementary exact-value comparison

**Compared values:** every string leaf of a fixture's model-facing `input` of at most 80 characters. For
extraction, also every string value in its gold. The fields are:

| Class | Fields |
|---|---|
| CONV | each `answer_options` entry, and every entry of any other list-of-strings input field (such as slot lists) |
| EXTR | gold string values (the free-text extraction values) |
| SYNTH | none; its structured fields are closed vocabulary (roles and conclusions), and its texts are covered by the trigram and entity checks |
| PLAN | each `allowed_actions[].action` (also covered by the uniqueness rule) |
| RSRCH | each `sources[].lineage` |

**Normalization:** casefold and strip.

**Exclusions** (declared; nothing else):
- closed-vocabulary sets: `allowed_recommendations`, `allowed_conclusions`, `allowed_uncertainty_codes`, schema
  type strings, and planning uncertainty codes;
- structural-id fields: `claim_id`, `source_id`, observation, statement, step and evidence ids, and `addresses`;
- numbers and booleans;
- strings that fully match one of these frozen calendar and number patterns:
  - ISO date `\d{4}-\d{2}-\d{2}`;
  - clock time `\d{1,2}:\d{2}`;
  - an optional weekday name followed by an optional clock time;
  - a number with an optional unit or currency word from a frozen list of 40 units.

**Gate:** 0 shared values, within G-ROUTE4 (main and reserve) and against G-ROUTE3's fixtures under the same rule.
The report lists, per class, the fields and the number of values compared.

**Leading-word coverage (reported, not gated):** the named-entity detector is re-run with a neutral `a, ` prefix on
every string leaf, so sentence-initial capitalized words are also seen. Collisions are reported for the corpus
review.

## 8. Conversation rules

- **Exactly 4 options** in every fixture (declared change from G-ROUTE3's mix of 3 and 4).
- **Gold position:** within 1 per cell in each corpus. A′ cells are 1/1/1/1; B′ eligible cells are 7/7/7/7; the R4
  case is at position 1.
- **Reserves** match on position (§5), so replacements keep the balance.
- **One near-miss distractor per fixture:** an option satisfying all but one condition, so a partial reading picks
  a wrong option rather than an invalid one.

## 9. Research feature balance

See §3.
- P1–P8 are the construct patterns, relabelled from G-ROUTE3's S1–S8.
- `sls` holds in exactly half of every eligible cell, and crosses every pattern in B′.
- The allocation comes from the construct, not from G-ROUTE3's failure rates.
- The coverage gap (A′ sees 4 patterns per cell, B′ sees 8) is declared in the design.

## 10. Coding (excluded from G-ROUTE4; forward specification only)

Coding is deferred to its own experiment (D6). No G-ROUTE4 fixture, cell or call is coding. The structure below is
recorded so that experiment can start from it. **It is not used here.**

**Ruling table over a closed universe:** every Python built-in, and every method name of `str`, `list`, `dict`,
`set`, `tuple` and `int`. Columns: name, kind, verdict (allow or deny), and reason class. The reason classes are:
- pure;
- I/O;
- reflection or introspection;
- code execution;
- non-deterministic;
- mutation of non-local state;
- takes a callable argument.

**AST rules (primary control):**
- deny any load of a non-allowlisted built-in name;
- deny rebinding any built-in or allowlisted name (assignment, parameters, def, import-as, loop and comprehension
  targets, walrus);
- deny decorators and dunder access;
- deny callable arguments such as `key=`;
- exclude `format` and `format_map`.

**Containment:** a real sandbox with a restricted token, no network, and no filesystem outside a scratch folder.
**Certification:** a probe for every known bypass.

## 11. Escalation-relevant composition

- E1 is descriptive (D3). The blueprint **does not construct, select or tune any case to cause an operational
  rejection or trigger**, since that would be selection on expected outcomes.
- Escalation can only occur in cells where A′ qualifies at least 2 tiers, which is unknown until A′ runs.
- The composition relevant to E1 is simply the frozen B′ mix: 300 eligible cases across 15 cells.
- The planning figure (P(at least 5 escalations) ≈ 0.62–0.80) is in the design.

## 12. Audit-sample procedure (O1), frozen with the blueprint

`audit_sample.py`, reproduced here in words:
- `key = sha256(ascii(seal_commit_id) + ":" + ascii(fixture_id))`, as a lowercase hex digest.
- For each B′ cell, the `ceil(0.10 × cell size)` main fixtures with the smallest key are sampled: 3 per conversation
  cell, 2 per other eligible cell, 1 per R4 cell, **38 in total**.
- It runs only after the single seal commit, whose parent is the blueprint commit.
- Its output is committed before the first adjudication session.
- **Frozen test vector:** seal `0123456789abcdef0123456789abcdef01234567` over the synthetic cells in the script
  gives digest `75af7d1ad5d785b40c1a41f15e4d23fa7e987715530e8d9b64f4fec5a4b398c9`. The feasibility report
  re-checks it.
