# G-CORROB1-R2 contamination ledger

**Purpose:** document how r1 outcome-informed content was removed and why r2
primary items are not merely renamed replacements.

## Removed r1 items

| R1 item | Audit finding | R2 treatment |
|---|---|---|
| C01 | Directly encoded the correction to G-EVID1 I51 (`exactly n` entails `at least m` when n >= m). | Removed. R2 contains no exact-versus-at-least threshold construction. R03/R04 instead compare existential and universal population coverage with unresolved members. |
| C05-C06 | Close reproduction of G-EVID1 I54/I55's simple claim versus added unsupported conjunct. | Removed without a conjunction-family replacement. R2 does not use an added-conjunct boundary as primary evidence. |
| C18 | Operationalized the audit warning against inferring persistence from an older changeable state. | Removed. R2 temporal items use explicit validity/closure intervals and observed boundary membership, not persistence inference. |
| C19-C20 | Close reproduction of G-EVID1 I52/I53's current-versus-superseded contrast. | Removed. R2 has no supersession minimal pair; R17-R20 use explicit interval inclusion and exclusion. |

The removed content remains only in the immutable r1 candidate as development
evidence. It is absent from r2 primary and diagnostic scoring.

## Remaining limitation

The r2 author had access to G-EVID1 and the r1 audit. Therefore r2 is a clean
prospective revision, not a historically blind corpus. Its claims must remain
limited to this preregistered corpus. A later external replication should use a
corpus commissioned from an author blind to all G-EVID1/r1 outcomes.
