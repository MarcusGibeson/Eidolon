# Pre-Audit Authority Refinement

The initial offline pilot passed 11,582 deterministic checks, with two complete
80-observation trees identical across 327 files. Its candidate remains
unactivated and is superseded, never promoted by the new pilot.

Before the one independent audit, the G-CAL1 adapter was tightened to preserve
the generic laboratory's explicit non-synthetic live-transport boundary.
Non-Boolean mode flags, unmarked/synthetic live callables, and explicitly
synthetic live success receipts reject with the existing PROVENANCE_MISMATCH
event. No broad BaseException swallowing was added. Live collection remains
unavailable without a distinct G-CAL1 activation and CAL authorization.

The initial manifest and pilot/candidate evidence are preserved. Only draft
implementation pins in the new G-CAL1 manifest are refreshed; the corpus,
gold, schedule, requests, scientific allocation and historical artifacts are
unchanged. Final pilot and candidate evidence live under `preexecution/final`.
This is pre-audit implementation work, not a post-audit repair or execution.
